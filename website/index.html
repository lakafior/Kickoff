A better Kick chat in Safari.

Your screen stays awake, deleted messages stay readable, and links, emotes and fonts look the way they should.

What it does

Everything is switched on from one place: the rocket at the top of the chat. Turn off whatever you don't want.

Keeps the screen awake

Your Mac, iPhone or iPad won't dim or lock in the middle of a stream.

Deleted messages stay

Instead of “Deleted by a moderator” you see what was said, greyed out. Messages of timed-out and banned users too.

Link previews

Images, YouTube, TikTok and X posts open right in the chat, so you don't have to leave the stream.

7TV emotes

Channel and global 7TV emotes with name paints and badges, an emote picker and autocomplete.

Mentions and search

An @ inbox of messages that mention you or your chosen words, and search across the chat, including from:name.

A calmer chat

Highlight the streamer and your words. Collapse muted users, repeated spam and emote-only messages.

Apple fonts

San Francisco, New York or SF Mono in place of Kick's font, with + and − for text size.

Native player

Safari's own video player on channel pages: picture in picture, AirPlay, audio on the lock screen and keyboard shortcuts on Mac.

Popout and audio

A chat-only popout with stream info, and a listen button with a sleep timer for when you only want to hear it.

Turn it on in a minute

Safari extensions come as an app. Install Kickoff, then switch it on once.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions. On iOS 17: Settings › Safari › Extensions.
  2. Tap Kickoff, turn it on and set kick.com to Allow.
  3. Open a stream on kick.com and tap the rocket at the top of the chat.

Mac

  1. Open Kickoff and click Open Safari Settings, then turn on Kickoff.
  2. On kick.com, click Kickoff in Safari's toolbar and choose Always Allow on This Website.
  3. Click the rocket at the top of the chat.

It only touches kick.com.

Kickoff asks Safari for access to one site and nothing else. It has no account, no server and no analytics. Your settings and saved messages sync between your Mac, iPhone and iPad through your own iCloud.

It also blocks the ad and tracking services kick.com loads, so the page has less watching you, not more.
