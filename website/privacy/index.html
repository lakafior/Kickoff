Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026. Applies to the Kickoff app and Safari extension for Mac, iPhone and iPad, and to this website.

Kickoff collects no personal data. It has no server, no user accounts and no analytics. Nothing you see or type is sent to the developer, and there is no system anywhere that could receive it.

What the extension can access

Kickoff asks Safari for access to kick.com only. On kick.com pages it reads the page, mainly the chat, so it can add its features: showing deleted messages, highlighting mentions, previewing links, adding emotes and so on. All of this happens in Safari on your device. Kickoff cannot see any other website you visit.

The Kickoff app explains how to turn the extension on, and has a player for Kick streams, opened by Player in the extension's view switcher. The app connects to the network only while that player shows a stream, and only to Kick (see below). It cannot see what you do in Safari.

What is stored, and where

Everything Kickoff keeps is stored on your device, in Safari or in the app:

  • In the extension's own storage: your settings (which features are on, chat font and size, highlighted and hidden words, muted and highlighted users), the phrases you saved, the notes you wrote about other users, and your mentions inbox (the latest 50 highlighted messages per channel).
  • In kick.com's site storage: a cache of 7TV emotes, name paints and badges, which emotes you use most (for autocomplete), each channel's !commands with the bot's last answer, the player's volume, whether you hid the message box, and when you last saw each channel's chat (so missed messages can be filled in).
  • In the Kickoff app: the player's quality, volume and whether its window floats on top. Nothing about what you watch.
  • In memory only: recent chat messages, so ones a moderator deletes can still be shown. They are gone when you close the tab.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your settings (except text size), your saved phrases, your notes about users, which 7TV emotes you use most and the channels' learned !commands are also kept in your own iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so your Mac, iPhone and iPad share them. That storage belongs to your Apple Account: the developer has no access to it and cannot read it. The mentions inbox, the caches and everything in memory stay on the device. Nothing else is uploaded anywhere by Kickoff.

Who Kickoff talks to

To do its job, Kickoff makes requests from your browser, or from the app's player, to these services. They receive what any website you open receives, such as your IP address. Kickoff tells them only what it is looking up (a channel, an emote set, a link) and nothing about you.

  • Kick (kick.com, web.kick.com, files.kick.com and its video service *.live-video.net): stream details, chat history after the page slept, the video and audio stream for the native player and the app's player, and the streamer's picture for the lock screen.
  • 7TV (7tv.io): emotes, name paints and badges.
  • YouTube, TikTok and FxTwitter (api.fxtwitter.com): titles and thumbnails for link previews, only for links someone posts in chat.
  • Image hosts: when someone posts a link to an image, the preview is loaded from wherever that image is hosted.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Blocking ads and trackers

With Block ads & trackers on (the default), Kickoff tells Safari to block requests that kick.com makes to advertising and analytics services, such as Google's ad and analytics domains, Amazon ads, Datadog, Datazoom and Mux. This uses Safari's built-in blocking rules: Kickoff does not see or keep the blocked requests. You can turn it off in the settings panel.

What the developer can see

Only what Apple reports to any developer with an app on the App Store: the number of downloads, crashes and ratings, in aggregate. This comes from Apple, not from Kickoff, and does not identify you. If you have turned off Share With App Developers in your device's analytics settings, Apple does not include your device at all.

No third-party code

Kickoff contains no advertising, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter and no tracking pixel. The extension is plain JavaScript and CSS, and the entire source is published at github.com/lakafior/Kickoff under the Mozilla Public License 2.0, so you can check all of this.

This website

This site is static HTML hosted on Cloudflare Pages. It sets no cookies and loads no trackers, ads or third-party fonts. A small script from smigi.net draws the window frame. Cloudflare processes requests to serve the page and may keep short-lived operational logs, as any web host does.

Deleting your data

Delete the Kickoff app and Safari removes the extension and its storage. To clear what Kickoff kept in kick.com's site storage, remove kick.com's website data in Safari: on Mac, Safari › Settings › Privacy › Manage Website Data; on iPhone and iPad, Settings › Apps › Safari › Advanced › Website Data. Because the developer holds nothing, there is no request to send and nobody to ask.

Children

Kickoff is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Not affiliated with Kick

Kickoff is an independent project. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Kick. What Kick does with your data is covered by Kick's own privacy policy.
