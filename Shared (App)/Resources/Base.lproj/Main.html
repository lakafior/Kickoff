Kickoff

A better Kick.com chat in Safari.

  • Keeps the screen awake while you watch.
  • Shows deleted messages, greyed out, instead of “Deleted by a moderator”.
  • Link previews, 7TV emotes, mentions inbox and chat search.
  • Apple fonts and adjustable text size.
  • Chat-only popout, background audio and ad & tracker blocking.
  • A player close to live: tap Player in the switcher at the top of the chat, with picture in picture and AirPlay.

Turn it on

  1. Open Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions.
    On iOS 17: Settings › Safari › Extensions.
  2. Tap Kickoff, turn it on and set kick.com to Allow.
  3. Open a stream on kick.com and tap the rocket at the top of the chat for settings.
